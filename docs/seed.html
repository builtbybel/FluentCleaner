How to use it

  1. Copy the seed with the button above.
  2. Open FluentCleaner and click Share selection at the bottom left.
  3. It's already in the box. Click Apply and analyze.

Nothing is deleted until you click Run Cleaner yourself.

The seed never leaves your browser. It sits after the # in the link, which isn't sent to any server.
